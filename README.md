# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Using SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing pizza sales data using SQL to identify key business insights, understand customer purchasing behavior, and evaluate sales performance. The analysis helps uncover popular pizza products, revenue trends, and opportunities to improve business performance through data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance and revenue.
- Calculate total orders and quantities sold.
- Identify the best-selling pizzas based on sales and quantity.
- Analyze category-wise and size-wise pizza sales.
- Understand customer ordering patterns by hour.
- Calculate cumulative revenue and track sales trends.
- Generate actionable insights to support business decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **SQL**
- **MySQL**
- **Data Analysis**
- **Aggregate Functions**
- **Joins and Subqueries**
- **GROUP BY and ORDER BY**
- **Window Functions**

## 📊 Key Business Questions

1. What is the total revenue generated from pizza sales?
2. How many orders were placed?
3. What is the average order value?
4. Which pizzas generate the highest revenue?
5. Which pizza categories contribute the most to sales?
6. What are the most popular pizza sizes?
7. During which hours do customers place the most orders?
8. What is the cumulative revenue over time?
9. Which pizzas perform best in terms of quantity sold and total revenue?

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

- **Sales Performance Analysis:** Evaluated total revenue, orders, and quantities sold.
- **Best-Selling Products:** Identified popular pizzas by revenue and quantity.
- **Category Analysis:** Compared sales performance across pizza categories.
- **Customer Ordering Trends:** Examined hourly ordering patterns.
- **Revenue Analysis:** Calculated cumulative revenue and explored sales performance.
- **Business Insights:** Used SQL query results to identify trends and support informed decisions.
